# Testing with real planners

Owner: Ayush. Goal: show Crowd Kit to **2–3 people who actually run events** (college fest committees, gig promoters, comedy night organisers) and write down what confused them and what they'd pay for. Status: **not started.**

## Before the session

- Use a live run of an event close to theirs, or a pre-cached one so they don't wait. Fall back to `/sample` if keys aren't working.
- Ask permission to take notes. Don't record names in this repo: use "Planner A", "Planner B" and a one-line role.
- Don't type any of their personal details, or their attendees' details, into the app. Qloo inputs must be public venues, acts and themes only.

## Session script (20–30 min)

**1. Their world (5 min). Don't show the app yet.**
- Tell me about the last event you ran. How many people, where, who was the act?
- How did you decide the food stalls? Sponsors? Merch? Volunteer numbers?
- What went wrong, or what would you do differently?
- What tools do you use today: spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, past-year notes?

**2. First impressions (10 min). Hand them the app.**
- Ask them to fill in the form for a real or upcoming event of theirs (public venue and act only).
- Stay quiet while it runs. Note where they hesitate, what they click and what they ask.
- Then: "What is this plan telling you?" Let them explain it in their own words.

**3. Probe (10 min)**
- Which recommendation would you actually act on? Which would you ignore, and why?
- Do the source badges matter to you? Do you trust a `qloo` line more or less than a `web` one?
- Open **With vs without Qloo**. Which plan would you rather have? Why?
- Is anything missing that you'd need before using this for real?
- Would you pay for this? How much, and per event or per year? Who in your team would pay?

**4. Wrap-up**
- One thing you'd change?
- Can we quote you, anonymously, in our submission?
